# GlowGuide – Smart Skincare Search & AI Consultation Platform

> **GlowGuide** is a skincare cosmetic search and classification platform integrated with Affiliate Marketing. The highlight of the project is the integration of an **AI Skincare Consultant**, providing a deeply personalized experience to help users find the most suitable products for their specific skin conditions.

---

## 📌 Project Introduction

With thousands of skincare product lines on the market and complex ingredient lists, users often struggle to choose safe and effective products. *GlowGuide* was created to solve this problem by providing an in-depth search filter and a smart consultation system. 

Beyond simply displaying product information, the website acts as a "virtual dermatologist," ready to interact two-way to understand the user's skin condition, thereby providing accurate recommendations and connecting directly to reputable sellers.

---

## ✨ Website Objectives
SkinMatch is designed to address common difficulties users face when choosing skincare products:
- Users may not know whether a product is suitable for their skin type.
- Product ingredient lists and their practical benefits can be difficult to understand.
- Many products appear similar but differ in price, brand, formulation, and usage experience.
- Product information is often distributed across multiple websites and e-commerce platforms.
- Users may need personalized advice but do not always have access to a skincare specialist.
- It can be difficult to identify suitable products for concerns such as oily skin, dry skin, sensitive skin, acne, hyperpigmentation, or enlarged pores.
The website is not only a product directory. It is also a decision-support platform that helps users discover and select skincare products more effectively.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

### 1. In-depth Skincare Search & Filter System
*   **Filter by skin type:** Users can categorize products specifically for oily, dry, combination, normal, or sensitive skin.
*   **Filter by skin concerns:** Supports finding products that address specific conditions such as: inflammatory acne, hyperpigmentation, large pores, anti-aging, skin barrier recovery, etc.
*   **Detailed product information:** Clearly displays origin, benefits, ingredient lists (with safety assessments), pros/cons, and usage instructions.

### 2. AI Chatbot – Virtual Skincare Consultant
This is the core feature that sets GlowGuide apart. The AI Chatbot does not just answer based on pre-written scripts but operates flexibly based on a vast database of cosmetics on the market:
*   **Comprehensive knowledge:** Can consult on products that are already available or *not yet updated* on the website.
*   **Proactive interaction (Follow-up Questions):** If the user asks a general question, the AI will proactively ask follow-up questions to gather more information. 
    *   *Example:* Customer asks *"Can I use ABC brand for oily skin?"*, the AI will reply *"Which specific ABC product line are you interested in? Do you currently have inflammatory acne?"* to provide the most practical advice.
*   **Flexible analysis & recommendations:** Warns about the risk of irritation or pore-clogging if the product inquired about is unsuitable for the current skin condition, while automatically recommending more optimal alternative products available on the platform.

#### Interactive Demo (AI Chatbot Sample)

**👤 User:** *"My skin is oily and acne-prone, can I use Cerave moisturizer?"*

**🤖 AI Consultant:** *"Hello! CeraVe has many different moisturizer lines. The Moisturizing Cream (large tub) contains very high moisturizing ingredients; using it on oily, acne-prone skin can cause clogged pores.* 
*👉 To give you a more accurate recommendation, are you experiencing inflammatory acne or just hidden acne?* 
*If you need a lightweight hydrating moisturizer, I recommend checking out the **CeraVe PM Facial Moisturizing Lotion**, or gel-based products like **La Roche-Posay Effaclar Mat**. Would you like to see the detailed ingredients for these products?"*

### 3. Affiliate Marketing Integration
*   Provides direct purchasing links (Affiliate Links) to official stores on e-commerce platforms (Shopee Mall, Lazada Mall, TikTok Shop, etc.).
*   Helps users easily compare prices and make purchasing decisions right after receiving useful consultation.
